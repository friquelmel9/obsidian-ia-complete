# Genetic Algorithms

Genetic algorithms are a kind of [[Local Search Algorithms]] that works based on the [Natural Selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_selection). 

Algorithms based on genetic algorithms generally share the concept of population, generations, and mutation or crossing. Some algorithms are:

- The [[Evolution Algorithm]] is the implementation of the genetic algorithm for [[Constraint Satisfaction Problem|CSP]] (Discrete and Restrictions). 
- [[Ant Colony Optimization]] is an algorithm based on the [Pheromone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pheromone) leaved on their tracks to find food.
